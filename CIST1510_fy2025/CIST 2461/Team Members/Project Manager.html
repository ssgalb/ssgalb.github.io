"Project Management"

Co-Project Manager | Natasha

Natasha serves as a Co‑Project Manager for Dream Team Consulting, supporting the coordination, planning, and execution of multi‑department project activities. She ensures that project timelines, communication channels, and operational workflows remain aligned with organizational goals.

With a strong background in administrative leadership and project coordination, Natasha excels at organizing complex tasks, maintaining documentation accuracy, and ensuring that project milestones are met. Her ability to manage cross‑functional teams makes her a key contributor to project success.

Co-Project Manager | Richard

Richard serves as a Co‑Project Manager for Dream Team Consulting, where he supports project oversight, workflow optimization, and cross‑department coordination. His role focuses on ensuring that project objectives are met through structured planning, risk awareness, and effective communication.

Richard brings a strong technical and operational background, including experience with Cisco systems, network segmentation, secure routing architectures, and multi‑VLAN environments. His technical insight strengthens project planning by ensuring that infrastructure, security, and communication systems remain reliable and scalable.

Beyond technology, Richard contributes to business intelligence efforts by applying data modeling, operational analytics, and performance dashboards to support decision‑making. His experience in workflow optimization and logistics management provides practical value to project execution.

Richard’s programming experience includes Java development, JavaFX UI engineering, Gradle build systems, C++ architecture, TLS-secured server configuration, and API deployment. He continues to expand his expertise through advanced coursework in data center operations and Java networking.

At Dream Team Consulting, Richard’s mission is to ensure that project operations run smoothly, efficiently, and securely—supporting every department with structured planning and dependable project oversight.
