"Information Services Division"

Information Services Manager Sheldon Smith

Sheldon Smith serves as the Information Services Manager for Dream Team Consulting, where he leads all activities related to the flow, protection, and operational continuity of information across the organization's data center driven infrastructure. His work ensures that communication systems, internal networks, customer facing platforms, and mission critical applications remain secure, resilient, and fully operational.

Sheldon brings a strong analytical background shaped by his research experience with the Albany County Comptroller's Office, where he conducted shared-services analysis and contributed to municipal governance initiatives. His academic work spans fiscal federalism, global central banking, corporate finance, and financial economics; giving him a unique ability to bridge technical operations with strategic, data-driven decision-making.

Technically, Sheldon specializes in Cisco systems, IP addressing, subnetting, and network segmentation. His expertise includes designing and maintaining critical infrastructure, implementing secure routing architectures, and ensuring high-availability communication systems within data-center environments. He is experienced in applying Cisco-based methodologies to multi-VLAN environments, transport-layer networking, and enterprise-grade access control systems.

Sheldon's skill set extends into business intelligence, where he leverages data modeling, operational analytics, and performance dashboards to support strategic decision-making. His operational background includes leadership experience in the home-improvement industry, where he managed logistics, workflow optimization, customer operations, and large-scale project coordination giving him a practical, real-world understanding of how technology supports business outcomes.

Professionally, Sheldon is a programming and network specialist with experience supporting government-affiliated environments. His technical capabilities include Java development, JavaFX UI engineering, Gradle build systems, C++ trading engine architecture, TLS-secured server configuration, and secure API deployment. He is currently advancing his expertise through Data Center I (CIST 2461) and continued study in Java networking, sockets, and server deployment.

At Dream Team Consulting, Sheldon's mission is clear: ensure that information flows securely, efficiently, and intelligently empowering every department, every system, and every customer interaction with reliability and confidence.
