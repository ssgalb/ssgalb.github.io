Information Services is responsible for leading all activities related to the flow of information. This department overlaps with Security by ensuring that all communication systems are protected and fully operational for team members, employees, and customers. Information Services supports both internal and external access, including employees using the Dream Team’s intranet and customers accessing the Dream Team’s extranet. Information Services ensures that all Dream Team systems remain operational at all times. This includes managing the routing of information, maintaining communication infrastructure, and ensuring that all payment systems remain functional and secure.
